5 Services Description

DogTalk is a utility app that helps dog owners better understand and care for their pets. The App stores all your personal content (profiles, health records, care logs) locally on your device. The AI analysis feature is powered by Google Gemini API.

  • Dog Behavior Analysis: Ethology-based analysis of dog body language and behavior from photos, powered by Google Gemini AI. The analysis draws on principles of ethology (the scientific study of animal behavior) to interpret posture, facial expressions, and body signals.
  • Dog Profiles: Create and manage profiles for your dogs locally on your device, including breed information, health records, vaccination history, and veterinary documents.
  • Care Tracking & Reminders: Log your dog's water intake, food, activities, and daily wellness. Set customizable reminders for feeding, hydration, and activities. Reminders are a convenience tool designed to help you stay on top of your dog's routine. They are not medical alerts and do not monitor your dog's actual condition.
  • Tips & Lifehacks: Curated dog care tips, training advice, and personalized recommendations.
  • Training Achievements: Track training milestones through gamified achievement cards.

6 AI-Generated Content and Limitations

The App uses Google Gemini AI to analyze dog behavior based on ethological principles. This section describes the capabilities and limitations of this feature.

6.1 Informational Purpose Only

  • AI-generated analysis and recommendations are for informational and educational purposes only. They are designed to help you learn about canine body language and behavior patterns.
  • The analysis is not a veterinary diagnosis, not a health assessment, and not a substitute for professional veterinary care.
  • You shall not represent AI-generated content as professional veterinary advice or as human-created expert analysis.

6.2 No Medical or Emergency Reliance

  • The App cannot detect medical conditions, pain, illness, or emergencies. If your dog shows signs of distress, injury, illness, or unusual behavior, contact a veterinarian immediately regardless of what the App displays.
  • AI may misinterpret, underestimate, or fail to identify signs that a trained veterinarian or behaviorist would recognize. This is an inherent limitation of image-based AI analysis.
  • The Company is not responsible for any harm, injury, or loss resulting from reliance on AI-generated analysis, delayed veterinary care, or failure to seek professional advice.

6.3 Accuracy Limitations

  • AI outputs may contain inaccuracies, errors, or incomplete interpretations. Results depend on photo quality, angle, lighting, and visible body language signals.
  • Ethological interpretation of behavior is context-dependent. The AI does not have access to your dog's history, environment, or full behavioral context.
  • The same behavior may have different meanings in different contexts. The App provides general guidance, not definitive conclusions.

6.4 Reminders and Care Tools

  • Reminders (water, feeding, activity) are convenience tools that help you maintain a care routine. They are based on schedules you set and do not monitor your dog's actual needs or condition.
  • Reminders do not replace your judgment as a pet owner. Your dog's actual needs may differ from any schedule or recommendation the App provides.
