Getting Started

What is AI TV REMOTE?

AI TV REMOTE is a smart universal remote for your iPhone that lets you control compatible smart TVs over your local Wi-Fi network. It also includes a built-in AI assistant to help you find shows, get recommendations, and answer questions about content.

Which TVs are supported?

The app supports a wide range of smart TVs that expose standard network-control protocols, including many Amazon Fire TV, and other Wi-Fi enabled smart TV devices. Your TV and iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

How do I connect my TV?
  • Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Open the app and tap Search for devices.
  • Select your TV from the list and confirm the pairing prompt on your TV screen if one appears.
  • That's it — the remote is ready to use.
The app can't find my TV. What should I do?
  • Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (not guest / 5GHz-only if the TV is 2.4GHz-only).
  • Disable VPN on your iPhone while pairing.
  • Restart your router, TV, and the app.
  • Allow Local Network permission for the app in Settings → Privacy → Local Network.
  • If the issue persists, contact support with your TV model and iOS version.

AI Chat

What can the AI assistant do?

The AI assistant can recommend movies and TV shows, explain plots, suggest what to watch based on your mood, answer general questions, and help you navigate content. It is powered by the OpenAI API.

Is the AI always accurate?

No. AI responses may contain errors, outdated information, or hallucinated facts. Do not rely on the AI for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice. Always double-check important information.

Is my chat data private?

Your prompts are sent to OpenAI only to generate a response. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. We do not sell or share your chat content. For details see our Privacy Policy.

Should I share personal information with the AI?

No. Please do not send passwords, payment details, health data, government IDs, or other sensitive information to the AI chat.

Subscription & Billing

Is the app free?

AI TV REMOTE offers a free tier with core remote features. Advanced features (including unlimited AI Chat) require an auto-renewing subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  • Open Settings on your iPhone.
  • Tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Select AI TV REMOTE and tap Cancel Subscription.

You'll continue to have access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and request a refund for the purchase.

I subscribed on another device — how do I restore it?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Will my subscription auto-renew?

Yes. Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each period unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage auto-renewal in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Troubleshooting

The remote buttons don't work.
  • Confirm that the TV is still on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Reconnect to the TV from the device list.
  • Force-quit the app and reopen it.
  • Check for app updates in the App Store.
The app crashes or freezes.
  • Update to the latest version of the app and iOS.
  • Restart your iPhone.
  • Reinstall the app if the problem continues.
  • Send us your device model, iOS version, and a short description to support@aplexityai.com.
Why does the app ask for Local Network access?

AI TV REMOTE needs Local Network permission to discover your TV on Wi-Fi using Bonjour/mDNS. Without it, the app can't find or control your TV. This data stays on your local network.
